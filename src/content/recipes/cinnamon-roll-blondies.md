## Instructions

1.  Pretheat oven to 375F and grease a pan.

2.  Whisk together butter and both sugars, cinnamon, and salt, then eggs
    and vanilla extract. Fold in flour. Pour into a greased pan.

3.  Whisk together all ingredients for cinnamon swirl. Pour over blondie
    mix and swirl with a knife.

4.  Bake 20-30 minutes until the top and edges are crisp and the center
    no longer jiggles.

5.  Cool in the refrigerator.

6.  Cover with a thick layer of cream cheese icing.

7.  Slice and serve.
