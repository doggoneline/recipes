## Instructions

1.  Melt butter in a heavy pan over medium heat. Cook shallots in butter
    for 2 minutes. Stir in the rice; cook for another 2 minutes,
    stirring constantly, until lightly toasted. (Rice will be hot to the
    touch and smell grainy)

2.  Stir in 1 cup vegetable broth; continue stirring until liquid is
    almost completely absorbed. Repeat this process with remaining
    broth, stirring constantly.

3.  Stir in Parmesan and cook until rice is done and risotto is thick
    and creamy.
