  - { amount: 0.25, unit: "cup", item: "(10g) roughly chopped fresh mint leaves" }
  - { amount: 3, unit: "tbsp", item: "(45ml) extra-virgin olive oil", note: "plus more for drizzling" }
  - { amount: 4, unit: "ounce", item: "(110g) feta cheese" }
  - { item: "Salt and pepper" }
---

## Instructions

1.  Place watermelon and cucumber chunks in a large bowl. Finely chop
    lemon zest. Add lemon juice and half of zest to bowl with
    watermelon. Add mint and oil. Toss until evenly dressed. Season
    lightly to taste with salt and pepper.

2.  Transfer salad to a wide, shallow bowl or a large plate and spread
    out evenly. Crumble feta over the top. Sprinkle with remaining lemon
    zest. Drizzle with more olive oil and a few grinds of black pepper.
    Serve immediately.
