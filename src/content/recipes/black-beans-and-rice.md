## Instructions

1.  Heat 2 tbsp oil. Add onion, bell pepper, and serrano and cook until
    onion is soft and starting to take on some color, about 5 minutes.
    Add garlic, cumin, oregano, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and black pepper.
    Stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in rice and cook until
    most of the grains turn chalky white, 1 to 2 minutes.

2.  Add black beans, stock, apple cider vinegar, and Worcestershire
    sauce and stir well. Bring to a boil, and then reduce heat to low.
    Cover the pot and cook until the rice is completely cooked, stirring
    after 10 minutes or so, 15 to 20 minutes total. Garnish with sliced
    green scallions.
