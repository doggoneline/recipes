## Instructions

1.  Place lentils and broth in a small saucepan; bring to a boil. Reduce
    heat; simmer, covered, until tender, about 30 minutes.

2.  Preheat oven to 350°. Line a 9x5-in. loaf pan with parchment,
    letting ends extend up sides. Coat paper with cooking spray.

3.  In a large skillet, heat oil over medium heat; saute onion and
    garlic until tender.. Stir in herbs. Transfer to a large bowl to
    cool.

4.  Add cheese, rice, eggs, seasonings and lentils to vegetables; mix
    well. Transfer to prepared loaf pan. Mix ketchup and water; spread
    over loaf.

5.  Bake for 45-50 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before slicing
