## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 400 degrees

2.  Add tomatoes and garlic to a 3 quart baking dish. Toss with olive
    oil and a few pinches of salt. Place feta in the center of tomatoes
    and turn to coat with oil.

3.  Bake in center of preheated oven for 30 minutes.

4.  Increase oven to 450. Bake until tomatoes and cheese are starting to
    brown slightly, about 10 minutes longer.

5.  Meanwhile boil pasta to al dente in salted water. Save some pasta
    water

6.  Smash the tomatoes, garlic, oil and feta together.

7.  Toss pasta in with mixture along with basil, parsley and red pepper
    flakes.

8.  Thin mixture with a few tablespoons of pasta water at a time to
    reach desired consistency. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
