## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 450 degrees.

2.  Combine the flour, milk, and garlic powder in a bowl. Put the panko
    and crushed red pepper along with some salt in another bowl. Dip the
    cauliflower into the wet batter and then into the panko. Put them on
    a baking sheet in a single layer. Bake until brown and crisp, about
    28-30 minutes.
