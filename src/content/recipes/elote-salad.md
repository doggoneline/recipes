## Instructions

1.  Heat a large skillet on medium high heat and melt the butter. Add
    the corn and sauté, stirring occasionally until lightly browned.
    Turn off the heat.

2.  Combine the dressing ingredients and add it to the corn along with
    cotija cheese. Serve warm.
