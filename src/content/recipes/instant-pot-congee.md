## Instructions

1.  Peel and slice the ginger, and crush the cloves of garlic.

2.  Add all ingredients to the Instant Pot.

3.  Close and lock the lid of the Instant Pot. Turn the steam release
    valve to the "sealing" position. Cook at high pressure for 20
    minutes.

4.  After 20 minutes, the pot will switch to the "keep warm" setting and
    begin to drop in pressure. Allow the pot to reduce in pressure
    naturally (20 minutes) without opening the steam release valve. When
    the float valve falls back to the down position, open the steam
    valve, and then the lid.

5.  Stir and taste, add salt as needed.
