## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 425°F. Wash and dry broccoli thoroughly.

2.  Cut into medium-sized florets and coat well with olive oil, salt,
    and pepper (and any additional spices)

3.  Spread onto rimmed baking sheet.

4.  Bake for 10 minutes, flip, and bake for 10 more minutes. Serve
    immediately.
