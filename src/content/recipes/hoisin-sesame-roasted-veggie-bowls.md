## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 425F. Wash and dry all produce. Trim and thinly
    slice scallions, separating whites from greens. Peel and mince or
    grate ginger. Dice sweet potato into ½ inch pieces. Core, deseed,
    and dice bell pepper into ½ inch pieces.

2.  Melt 2 tbsp butter in a small pot over medium heat. Add scallion
    whites and 1 tbsp ginger; cook and stir until fragrant, about 1
    minute. Add rice, 1 ¼ cups water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a
    boil then cover and reduce heat to low (just enough to keep it
    simmering). Cook until rice is tender, 15-18 minutes. When rice is
    cooked, add another tablespoon of butter and stir into rice. Keep
    covered and off heat until ready to serve.

3.  Meanwhile, in a bowl, toss sweet potato with a drizzle of oil and
    about 1 tbsp of hoisin sauce, salt, and pepper. Spread out on one
    side of a baking sheet. Toss bell pepper with oil, salt, and pepper
    then spread on the other side of the baking sheet. Roast until
    veggies are just tender but not fully cooked, about 15 minutes.

4.  While the veggies roast, in a small bowl, combine mayo, sour cream,
    soy sauce, and as much sriracha as you like (I used about 2 tsp but
    start small and taste, adding until it's at the level you like)

5.  Once veggies have roasted for about 15 minutes, remove the baking
    sheet from the oven and using a spatula, toss them on the sheet tray
    with another tbsp of hoisin and some sesame seeds (save some to put
    on top at the end). Return to oven until the veggies are cooked
    through, about 5-8 minutes.

6.  Fluff the rice with a fork and taste, season with more salt if
    needed. Divide rice between bows. Top with sweet potatoes and bell
    pepper. Drizzle everything with sriracha soy mayo sauce. Top that
    with scallion greens and sesame seeds.
