## Instructions

1.  Finely grate lemon zest, set enough aside to garnish; set the rest
    aside. Cut lemons in half and squeeze out enough juice to yield at
    least 2 tbsp into a small bowl; set aside.

2.  Cook pasta in a large pot of boiling, heavily salted water, stirring
    occasionally, until very al dente (pasta will finish cooking in the
    sauce).

3.  Just before pasta is finished cooking, scoop out 1½ cups of pasta
    water; set aside. Strain the rest of the water from the pasta.

4.  Add cream and lemon zest to the pot and cook over medium heat,
    stirring often, until liquid is just beginning to simmer. Reduce
    heat to medium-low. Stir in butter 1 tbsp at a time until melted and
    sauce is creamy and emulsified. Add a few tbsp of pasta water to
    cream sauce and return to medium heat. Transfer spaghetti to the pot
    with sauce (it's okay if a little water comes along with it). Cook,
    tossing often and adding Parmesan little by little, until cheese is
    melted and sauce is creamy. If the sauce looks tight, add 1--2 tbsp
    pasta cooking liquid. Stir in lemon juice; season with pepper and
    more salt, if needed.

5.  Cook down to the desired thickness.

6.  Divide pasta among bowls. Season with pepper, then top with reserved
    lemon zest.
