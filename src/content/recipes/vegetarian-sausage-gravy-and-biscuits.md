## Instructions

1.  Heat butter in a medium skillet over medium-high heat and cook the
    onions 8-10 minutes until golden and beginning to brown.

2.  Add garlic and veggie sausage. Cook until browned.

3.  Sprinkle flour over the mixture and stir to incorporate, cooking for
    another minute.

4.  While stirring constantly to prevent lumps, pour in milk and heavy
    cream. Stir until smooth.

5.  Add vegetable bouillon, salt, pepper and cayenne. Bring to a simmer
    and stir until thickened.

6.  Add parsley, sage and thyme and simmer for another 2 minutes. Add
    salt and pepper to taste.

7.  Serve immediately over the biscuits. Garnish with some chopped fresh
    parsley.
