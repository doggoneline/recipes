## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 375°F. Heat butter and olive oil in a deep saute pan
    over medium heat. Add the onions, toss to coat in butter and oil.
    Cover the pot, Reduce heat to medium-low and let onions cook 15 - 20
    minutes. Start stirring sooner if they're browning or sticking.

2.  Uncover the pot, raise heat to medium-high and add salt. Cook
    onions, stirring every few minutes (check more frequently later in
    cooking) until the onions are brown and soft, about 20 minutes.
    Reduce heat to medium once any water has cooked off.

3.  Add garlic to the onions and cook, stirring, one minute. Add sherry
    and cook until evaporated. Add farro and cook for 2 minutes. Add
    lentils and 6c broth + thyme. Bring the mixture to a simmer. If the
    pan isn't ovenproof, transfer it at this point to a casserole dish.

4.  Cover the pan and bake, stirring about halfway through, until the
    lentils and farro are tender, 40 to 50 minutes. If all of the liquid
    has absorbed, either at the midway through point or at the end, add
    another 1/4 to 1/2 cup splash of broth. (Looking for a risotto-like
    looseness or light "slosh" in the pan) Fish out thyme stems.

5.  To finish, sprinkle the dish with gruyere and broil until browned on
    top.
