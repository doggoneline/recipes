## Instructions

1.  Finely dice the onion. Peel and finely chop the carrots. Mince the
    garlic.

2.  In a large pot, add the olive oil and heat over medium. Add the
    onion, carrot, and garlic and cook 3 to 4 minutes until just
    translucent. Add the paprika, cumin, and lentils and stir 1 minute.
    Add the canned tomatoes, vegetable broth, sausage, and kosher salt.

3.  Bring to a simmer. Simmer covered, stirring occasionally, for 20
    minutes. Remove lid and cook for an additional 10 to 15 minutes
    until the lentils are tender.

4.  Remove from heat. Taste and season with salt and pepper as
    necessary. For best flavor, garnish with Parmesan cheese.
