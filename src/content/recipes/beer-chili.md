## Instructions

1.  In a large heavy bottomed pot, brown the impossible in olive oil.
    Season with salt and pepper. Once browned, remove and set aside.

2.  In the same pot, add the chopped onions. Cook until slightly
    softened, then add the garlic and cook for a few minutes longer. Add
    the impossible back to the pan. Mix to incorporate.

3.  Add the kidney beans, crushed tomato, cumin, paprika, red pepper
    flakes, chili powder, water and stout. Bring to a boil. Cover and
    let simmer for an hour.

4.  Serve with shredded cheddar, sour cream and chives.
