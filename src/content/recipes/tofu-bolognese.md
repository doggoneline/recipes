## Instructions

1.  Prep tofu by freezing and then pressing.

2.  Preheat oven to 400F.

3.  Add 3 tbsp olive oil to a pan over medium heat. Add carrot and
    onion, cook for a few minutes to soften then add the garlic and cook
    everything for about 5 minutes.

4.  Add the tomato puree and cook for another minute or two. Season
    well.

5.  Add herbs and spices, stir, then deglaze pan with 2 tbsp balsamic
    vinegar.

6.  Add the crushed tomatoes, 2 cups of water, bouillon concentrate,
    tomato puree, and soy sauce. Season well, reduce heat, and leave to
    simmer.

7.  After tofu is pressed, grate on the coarse side of the grater. Place
    on a baking sheet with some oil and soy sauce, toss to coat. Bake
    15-20 minutes, turning often (important). Tofu will be done when
    browned and slightly crispy.

8.  When tofu is done cooking, remove from oven and add into the ragù
    mixture along with the milk and season to taste.
