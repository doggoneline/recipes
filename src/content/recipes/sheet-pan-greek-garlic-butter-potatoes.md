## Instructions

1.  Preheat oven to 425° F.

2.  On a large baking sheet, toss the potatoes and shallot with olive
    oil. Season with 2 tbsp dried oregano, paprika, chili flakes, salt,
    and pepper. Add the lemon halves. Roast for 20 minutes, or until
    tender.

3.  Remove the potatoes from the oven and add the slices of butter
    around the pan. Return to the oven for 20-25 minutes more

4.  To make the dressing. Combine 1/3 cup olive oil, red wine vinegar,
    parsley, 1 tbsp oregano, dill, pepperoncinis, garlic, and olives.

5.  Remove the charred lemon slices and garlic cloves from the baking
    sheet. Finely chop the lemon slices, rind and all, discarding any
    seeds. Add half of the lemon to the dressing. Chop/mash the garlic
    into a paste, stir the garlic into the dressing. Season with red
    pepper and salt. Taste adding more of the lemon as desired.

6.  Break the feta over the chicken, then add the dressing. Top with
    fresh herbs.
