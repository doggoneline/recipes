## Instructions

1.  Cook the tortellini in salted water according to the package
    instructions, and set aside, reserving a cup of the cooking water.

2.  Melt butter to a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and
    cook for another minute.

3.  Add the wine/stock and cook for a few minutes for the alcohol to
    bubble away.

4.  Stir in the heavy cream and parmesan. Cook for another minutes,
    stirring until all of the cheese has melted.

5.  Add the cooked tortellini and a splash of the reserved cooking
    water. Stir until the sauce coats the pasta and it has reached the
    desired consistency.

6.  Season to taste with salt and pepper.

7.  Serve with more parmesan and chopped parsley.
