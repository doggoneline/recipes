## Instructions

1.  Place the chickpeas in a mixing bowl and mash with a fork, leaving
    only a few beans whole.

2.  Add tahini, mustard, maple syrup, pickles, salt and pepper, to the
    bowl. Mix to incorporate. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.

3.  Toast bread if desired, and prepare any other desired sandwich
    toppings.

4.  Scoop the chickpea mixture onto one slice of bread, add desired
    toppings and top with a second slice of bread.
