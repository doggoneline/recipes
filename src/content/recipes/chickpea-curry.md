## Instructions

1.  Pulse serrano, garlic and ginger in a food processor until minced.
    Add onion; pulse until finely chopped (not watery)

2.  Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion
    mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3 to 5
    minutes.

3.  Add coriander, cumin and turmeric and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes.

4.  Pulse tomatoes in the food processor until finely chopped. Add to
    the pan along with salt. Reduce heat to maintain a simmer and cook,
    stirring occasionally, for 4 minutes.

5.  Add chickpeas and garam masala, reduce heat to a gentle simmer,
    cover and cook, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes more. Serve
    topped with cilantro, if desired.
