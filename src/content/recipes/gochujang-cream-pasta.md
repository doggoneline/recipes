## Instructions

1.  Cook and drain pasta. Set aside.

2.  Melt butter in a pot. Cook garlic until soft.

3.  Mix in gochujang, cream, soy sauce, and white pepper. Stir to
    combine.

4.  Add grated parmesan and stir until melted and thickened.

5.  Add cooked pasta to sauce and stir.
